01 / My beginnings

Where my story began

I was born on 4 June 2000 in Kyeshero, Rugando, Mboha Cell, Kanungu District, Uganda.

My life began in a family that had already experienced a great loss. My father died one week before I was born. I therefore grew up without the opportunity to experience his love and presence as a father. This is an important part of my family history and the story of my beginnings.

02 / My family

The woman who raised me

My mother, Turyatunga Margret, is one of the most important people in my life.

I grew up in Kyeshero under my mother's care. I am grateful for her strength and generous heart. She has helped and raised children in need, extending her care beyond her biological children.

I have one biological sister. Through my mother's kindness in caring for other children, our family grew to include five boys, including me, and two girls — seven children altogether.

My family story reminds me that family is shaped not only by blood relationships, but also by love, generosity and the decision to care for others.

03 / Learning

Education and personal development

I completed Senior Six. Financial challenges made it difficult for me to continue directly to university, but I remain interested in learning and developing practical skills.

My interests include business, financial management, technology, software development and entrepreneurship. I believe learning can continue through experience, independent study and opportunities that arise along the way.

04 / Entrepreneurship

Building a bigger vision

An important part of my journey is my work and ambition through COFISEE Microfinance.

My goal is to develop the business into a professional financial services institution. I want to strengthen its operations, serve customers responsibly, create employment opportunities for young people and contribute positively to Uganda.

I am interested in using technology and better systems to support financial records, loan management, repayments and reporting. This vision is a work in progress, and I hope to keep learning as I build.

05 / Looking ahead

The future I hope to create

I want to become financially independent, build sustainable businesses, improve my knowledge and create opportunities for other people. I also want to honour the sacrifices and kindness that have shaped my family story.

“My beginnings do not have to define the limits of my future. I can keep learning, build with purpose and create something meaningful for the generations that follow.”
